GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

What happens when someone scans my QR?

Your active profile opens and shows the name and phone number you agreed to share. The visitor can tap to call you.

Will my home address be visible?

No. Your address is used for delivery and is not shown on the public QR profile.

Does the QR track my vehicle?

No. This is a contact sticker, not a GPS tracker. It does not show live location.

Can I pay on delivery?

Cash on Delivery is available where delivery can be fulfilled. Online payment appears when the business has configured its payment gateway.
